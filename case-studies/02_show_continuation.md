# Do viewers watch another episode of the same show?

## Why I asked this question

Getting someone to watch one episode is only part of the goal. I also want to know whether they keep exploring the show.

## What I measured

For each episode, I count how many viewers watched a different episode of the same show afterward, before the reporting period ended.

## A simple example

If 100 people watched an episode and 35 later watched another episode of that show, the continuation rate would be 35%. This is an example, not a company result.

## How this helps the team

If few viewers continue, the team can review the episode ending, the link to another episode, and the recommendations shown to viewers.

## How the numbers are defined

A viewer continues when they watch a different episode of the same show after their first recorded watch of the episode being analyzed, within the selected dates. The percentage is calculated from that episode's distinct viewers.

This focuses on continued interest in the show. Viewing other shows and returning to the wider platform are separate measures.

## Data coverage

The reporting end date sets how long later viewing can be observed. Viewers arriving near that date have less follow-up time. Episode availability also matters when comparing results.

## What I would test next

Try a clearer next-episode suggestion and compare it with the current version. Give both groups the same amount of time to watch another episode.

## About the examples

The examples use made-up data. No company results are shared, and the suggested tests have not been carried out as part of this portfolio.
