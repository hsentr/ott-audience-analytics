# What do people watch first during the reporting period?

## Why I asked this question

The first show someone watches can help us understand how they start exploring the platform.

## What I measured

For each person, I find their first eligible on-demand program during the selected period. I then count how many people started with each program and whether they were registered users or guests at that time.

## A simple example

If someone watched Show A on Monday and Show B on Tuesday, Show A is their first program for that period—even if they used the platform before Monday.

## How this helps the team

This helps the team identify shows to consider for the home page, welcome experience, or content recommendations.

## How the numbers are defined

Each viewer is assigned to their first eligible on-demand program within each reporting period. Viewing time determines the order, with the view identifier used to break ties. Registered or guest status is taken from that first viewing record.

This answers how people start watching during a period. Identifying first-ever platform viewing is a separate acquisition question requiring the viewer's earlier history. The registration breakdown describes audience composition at the first watch.

## About the examples

The examples use made-up data. No company results are shared, and the suggested tests have not been carried out as part of this portfolio.
